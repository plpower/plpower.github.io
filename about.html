About

I love solving multi-faceted problems: ones with technical, design, and strategic implications. And I'm excited to apply my curiosity and creative problem solving toolkit to the world of emerging tech.

Currently

I'm a first-year MBA candidate at Harvard Business School (Class of 2028), looking at opportunities across product, strategy, and technology. Alongside school, I'm building Keeper, an app that helps people find service providers — starting with hair salons — they can actually trust.

I'm not trying to lock myself into one lane. If you think there's a fit across product, strategy, or engineering, I'd love to connect. Say hello.

Background

Most recently, I was Director of Solutions Engineering at Aeropay, a Chicago fintech company that offers bank-to-bank payments as a secure, lower-cost alternative to cards. I worked closely with enterprise partners and clients on payment integrations, built out the team's implementations function, and helped shape the product roadmap along the way.

Before Aeropay, I was a front-end experience developer on IBM's Interactive Experience team, building the UX for closed-loop referral systems used across IBM Community Health's healthcare clients.

I graduated cum laude from Northwestern University in 2021 with a degree in Computer Science and certificates in design and marketing. While there, I served as CTO of Student Holdings, a student-run nonprofit business, and volunteered web development and design work for local nonprofits through DISC.
