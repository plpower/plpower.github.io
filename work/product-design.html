Click 'n Cook

Product Designer · Spring 2018

A travel-friendly kosher cooking utensil set, designed over ten weeks with Chicago's Orthodox Jewish community. My role covered rapid prototyping and sketching, phone interviews and home visits, survey analysis, and managing the team's workstreams.

How might we make traveling easier for people who keep Kosher? Designated drawers and sinks make separating meat and dairy kitchenware straightforward at home. The uncertainty and inconsistency of rental kitchens makes doing the same while traveling far harder.

Photos from home visits with Orthodox Jewish families in the Chicago area.

We observed kosher kitchens on Northwestern's campus, visited homes and interviewed Chicagoland Orthodox Jewish families, conducted phone interviews with rabbis and other community members, and surveyed more than 200 congregants across local synagogues.

Design goals: lightweight and TSA-friendly for travel; an intuitive, color-coded handle-head joint; nonporous, kitchen-safe materials; and structural integrity under boiling water for Kashrut.

From initial concept sketches to final design.

The final design features a color-coded universal handle that attaches to interchangeable utensil heads with a magnetic snap-in joint — more compact to pack, with lightweight kitchen-safe material that keeps added baggage weight to a minimum.
